Permutation Feature Importance

Values indicate the mean decrease in model R² after randomly permuting a feature. Larger values indicate greater model dependence.

SHAP Feature Contribution Analysis

SHAP values show mean absolute feature contributions. Blue indicates a positive direction and red indicates a negative direction. Rejection targets are modelled in log(100 − R) space, so their directional interpretation is reversed.
